Vaal Cultivation Orb

Juatalotli's Triumph alters a Corrupted Vaal Unique Item, replacing one or more modifiers with Vaal Unique Modifiers.

Cultivated uniques have one or two of their modifiers replaced by cultivation modifiers which are listed in light red. The modifier that gets replaced on the unique are random.

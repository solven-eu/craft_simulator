Vaal Orb

Vaal Orb is a currency item that corrupts an item, modifying it unpredictably and preventing it from being further modified.

Mechanics

Vaal Orbs always entail a risk: they can greatly improve an item but they can also change it for the worse.

Item acquisition

Vaal Orb can drop anywhere.

Can also be obtained as a possible reward in The Trial of Chaos.
